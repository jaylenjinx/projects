A uni team project: a digital I Ching. Give it a shake and it shows you a hexagram.

Digiching Code

The firmware, running on an ESP32-S3. It reads motion from an MPU6050 to detect the shake, then draws the hexagram artwork and its meaning on a little 128×128 screen. Source on GitHub.
